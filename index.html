About me

Self-taught, hands-on,
obsessed with detail.

I started learning to code on my own in November 2025 — no bootcamp, no classroom, just curiosity and a laptop. What began as wondering how websites actually work turned into a habit I couldn't stop: rebuilding menus, redesigning layouts, breaking things and fixing them again until the page did exactly what I pictured.

Today I build clean, responsive websites for restaurants and small businesses, and design the visuals that go with them — thumbnails, posters, product shots and cinematic edits. I'm a certified backend developer too, so what I hand over isn't just good-looking. It's built to hold up.

If your business deserves better than a template, that's exactly the work I want.
